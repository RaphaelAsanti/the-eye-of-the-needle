The Eye of the Needle — A Personal Witness

This repository records a personal witness concerning a real place I have been, associated with the motifs of the wilderness and the eye of the needle. It is intentionally separate from the ebook, which is limited to material that can be validated through science, text, or scripture. Nothing in this repository functions as a key, a method, or an interpretive mechanism. It is simply a breadcrumb — a record of something known personally rather than derived from analysis.

The content here cannot be reasoned to, reconstructed, or reached from the architecture of the book. It is not part of the argument, not part of the structure, and not part of the textual discipline. It exists only to acknowledge that one personal experience informed my certainty, even though it does not belong inside the formal work.
Purpose

To document a place I have been that relates to the wilderness motif and the needle motif, without attempting to explain, justify, or derive anything from it. This repository is a witness, not an argument.
Why This Is Separate from the Ebook

The ebook excludes personal experiences by design. Its scope is strictly limited to what can be validated through measurable science or textual analysis. This repository exists outside that boundary. It is not intended to support, extend, or modify the book’s claims. It is simply here for readers who understand the architecture and wish to know that something personal stands behind it.
Boundary Statement

Nothing in this repository is symbolic or metaphorical.
Nothing here is meant to be used as a key.
Nothing here can be reached from the text.
It is only a witness to a place.
