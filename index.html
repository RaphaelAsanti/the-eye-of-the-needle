Witness Statement

The Eye of the Needle

Addendum: Why This Page Exists

In my other pages and in my ebook, I have kept the structure limited to material that can be validated scripturally or scientifically. Every claim in those works is tied to a text, a measurement, or a demonstrable mechanism. This page is different. It is included as an addendum because the event described here is not subject to external validation. It is not scriptural exegesis, not physics, not cosmology, and not measurement‑based geometry. It is a direct personal experience that occurred in 2019, and its relevance lies in the memories it unlocked and the context it provides for the rest of my work. For that reason, I have kept it separate from the main body of material, but available for those who want the full background.

Context

In 2019, while staying at the Thunderbird Motel in Las Vegas, New Mexico with my two daughters and their biological mother, I experienced an event that was not physical. Earlier that day, I played The Sound of Silence repeatedly, near tears, saying over and over: “They don’t want to go.” Later I learned this referred to the eternal paradise universe. The song itself describes what happens when the angel of music withdraws from this universe.

Departure

That night I had an out-of-body experience. I was taken straight upward, very far and very high, carrying a non-physical but real amalgam of every lie, rape, murder, deceit, and wrong, along with the emotional weight of every victim. Within it were small fragments of goodness, but as a whole it was repulsive.

Years later, after reading the Qur’an, I recognized this “huge sack of sins” as the same concept described in Qur’an 3:154 — “the contents of their hearts.”

The sack vanished the moment I arrived.

Arrival

I reached a place that is entirely spiritual, not located anywhere within this universe. It was utilitarian and unadorned. There were nine trapezoidal portals with rounded edges, grey-taupe in color, unmarked. I recognized them as portals to different creations:

  • one to this universe
  • one to the eternal paradise universe
  • seven to various hell universes

This recognition was the first memory that surfaced.

The Woman

A woman was there, kneeling, and expecting me. I recognized her but could not immediately recall how. My mind and soul were not aligned.

Later, after my soul‑level memories were processed through my prefrontal cortex, I understood who she is: the woman in the wilderness mentioned in Revelation, and my own female companion from the Eternal Paradise universe.

Our souls traveled together from our home in the Eternal Paradise universe, out of our lightbodies, and to the eye of the needle so my soul could be inserted into this universe. That is how my soul came to be here as a physical and spiritual orphan in a fetus of flesh — separated from my companion, separated from the place we originate, and an outsider in this universe.

This was not the first time we have done this, and it is not the first time I have had to come here. It is also not the first time she has had to sit and wait for me at that place.

She waits there for me to return.

The Place

The place itself is the eye of the needle referenced in both scripture traditions:

  • Jesus: “It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.”
  • Qur’an 7:40: “The gates of heaven will not be opened for them, and they will not enter Paradise until the camel passes through the eye of the needle.”

I did not read the Qur’an until years after the experience. This location is within the domain of God Almighty, where there is no material matter — only spirit, and where God Almighty controls all access.

Return

Exhausted, I collapsed on the floor. As I gathered my thoughts, the woman told me, “You have to go back.” I replied, “You don’t know how terrible that place is.” Her face softened with a look of concern I recognized. Immediately afterward, I was sent straight back down to the Thunderbird Motel.

Aftermath

The next morning I told the entire experience to my daughters’ biological mother. She was disinterested and only wanted to know where we were going for lunch.

It has now been seven years since this event.

Final Statement

Since that day, even though the flesh‑mind is terribly inefficient, I have been able to process many more memories from my soul through my prefrontal cortex, and it is a tedious process. I have distinct memories of the place I am from, exactly how and why I came here, and of my companion back home and our relationship.

As for the woman in the wilderness: If she were here, you would not be able to pry us apart with a crowbar.
